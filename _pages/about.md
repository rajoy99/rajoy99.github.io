# About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC) <img width="25px" src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/96/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago_circle_logo.svg" alt="University of Illinois Chicago logo">, where my research focuses on causal discovery (on Biomedical and Time series data), generative modeling, and machine learning for scientific and structured data.

## Research Interests

My current research interests include:

* Causal inference and causal discovery
* Diffusion and flow-based generative models, Score matching
* Synthetic Data Generation
* Learning with missing data
* Scientific machine learning
* Robust and efficient machine learning
* Physics Informed Neural Networks
